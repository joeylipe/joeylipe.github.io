# LEGO Collection Analysis
![Status: Data Acquisition](https://img.shields.io/badge/Status-Data--Acquisition-orange)

### **The Project**
I am currently auditing a legacy collection found in storage. Between the bricks, the tub, and the instruction manuals, the total weight is approximately 48 lbs. This is a manual "Search and Discovery" project to see what’s actually in the bin and complete the vintage sets I know are hidden inside.

### **The Process**
I am currently in the **Physical-to-Digital** phase:
1. **Sorting:** Breaking the bulk down by part type and color.
2. **Cataloging:** Entering unique IDs and quantities into a master Google Sheet.
3. **Identification:** Finding "Anchor Parts" to match the loose bricks back to their original sets.

---

> **"This project is about the grind of data integrity—taking 48 lbs of raw basement data and building a verifiable system from the ground up."**
